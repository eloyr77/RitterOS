RitterOS's C++ libraries (43c): /system/<arch>/lib/libc++.so.1,
libc++abi.so.1 and libunwind.so.1.

They are LLVM's libc++, libc++abi and libunwind (LLVM 22.1.8), built
unchanged from the sources Zig 0.17.0 ships (lib/libcxx, lib/libcxxabi,
lib/libunwind, and lib/libcxx/libc, LLVM-libc's parts libc++ uses), the
pinned toolchain scripts/setup-toolchain.sh installs into tools/zig. Only
their configuration is RitterOS's: user/cxx/__config_site and the build
rules in the Makefile ("C++").
