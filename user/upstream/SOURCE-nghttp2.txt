RitterOS's nghttp2 (46c): /system/<arch>/lib/libnghttp2.so.14 (HTTP/2's
framing, HPACK and its sessions, the library only) with the headers
/system/include/nghttp2/.

It is nghttp2 1.71.0 by Tatsuhiro Tsujikawa and its contributors, built
unchanged from its source archive (https://github.com/nghttp2/nghttp2/
releases/download/v1.71.0/nghttp2-1.71.0.tar.xz, SHA-256 in
user/upstream/versions, as the project's checksums.txt gives it), which
scripts/setup-toolchain.sh downloads into tools/src: none of its sources
are kept in this repository. Only the build is RitterOS's: its own CMake
through the SDK's sdk/ritteros.cmake (ENABLE_LIB_ONLY, shared), in the
Makefile's "nghttp2, brotli and libpsl" part.

Licence: the MIT licence (user/upstream/LICENCE-nghttp2.txt, nghttp2's
COPYING; installed as /system/doc/licences/nghttp2.txt), compatible with
the GPL; taking it in was agreed for item 46 (2026-11-12).
Upstream: https://nghttp2.org, https://github.com/nghttp2/nghttp2
