RitterOS's OpenSSL (46b): /system/<arch>/lib/libcrypto.so.3 and
libssl.so.3, the program /system/<arch>/bin/openssl, and the headers
/system/include/openssl/.

It is OpenSSL 3.5.9 (the 3.5 long-term support series) by the OpenSSL
Project, built unchanged from its source archive
(https://github.com/openssl/openssl/releases/download/openssl-3.5.9/
openssl-3.5.9.tar.gz, SHA-256 in user/upstream/versions), which
scripts/setup-toolchain.sh downloads into tools/src: none of its sources
are kept in this repository. Only the build is RitterOS's: its own
Configure with a target of RitterOS's (user/upstream/openssl.conf:
upstream's base Unix template, pthreads, dlopen, the assembly for x86-64
and AArch64), the options in the Makefile's "OpenSSL" part (no loadable
modules: no legacy provider, no engines; OPENSSLDIR /system/config/openssl,
whose cert.pem is a link to /system/certs/cacert.pem), and the program's
help line (user/upstream/openssl-help.c, linked into it).
